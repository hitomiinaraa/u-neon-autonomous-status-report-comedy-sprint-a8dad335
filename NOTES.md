# Revision notes

A weekly update feels honest when it admits a wobble instead of polishing it away.
Renaming a miss gives it a second life, and the joke is pretending that was the plan all along.
Counting finished work first sets up a tidy picture, then the last clause quietly knocks it sideways.
The voice stays calm and clerical, which makes the sideways move land harder.
Short sentences keep the rhythm brisk; nothing here needs a flourish to work.
The humour comes from the gap between the tidy numbers and the messy reality behind them.
A reader who has ever filed a Friday update will recognise the dodge at once.
The revision touched only these notes and one comment; the line itself stayed exactly as built.
